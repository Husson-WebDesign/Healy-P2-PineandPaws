Gentle, Stress-Free Pet Grooming in Bangor

Gentle care. Professional grooming. Happier pets.

At Pine & Paws Pet Grooming, every pet is treated with patience, care, and respect. Our goal is to make grooming as comfortable as possible while helping pets look and feel their best. From routine bathing and brushing to full grooming appointments, our staff takes the time to understand each pet's individual needs.

Our Commitment to Pet Safety

The comfort and safety of every pet comes first. Grooming services may be modified or stopped if continuing could cause excessive stress or create a safety concern for the pet or grooming staff.

What to Expect During Your Visit

A simple step-by-step process designed for your pet's comfort

1. Prepare Inquiry

Tell us about your pet's breed, size, and scheduling preferences using our demonstration inquiry form.

2. Discuss Needs

We review your pet's coat condition, health considerations, and grooming history prior to starting.

3. Gentle Grooming

Your pet experiences a quiet, attentive grooming session with rest breaks scheduled as needed.

4. Pick Up & Care Tips

Pick up your clean, happy pet and receive individualized maintenance advice from our staff.
