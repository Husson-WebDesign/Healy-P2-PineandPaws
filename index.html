Pine and Paw Pet Grooming

Gentle care. Professional grooming. Happier pets.

The salon provides professional grooming services in a clean, calm, and safety-focused environment.

The business was created around the idea that grooming should be more than simply improving a pet's appearance. Grooming should also support coat health, comfort, hygiene, and the overall well-being of the animal.

About Us

Pine & Paws Pet Grooming was established to provide local pet owners with professional grooming in an environment built around patience, cleanliness, and respectful animal handling.

The salon recognizes that pets respond differently to grooming. Some pets are comfortable immediately, while others may need additional time and reassurance.
