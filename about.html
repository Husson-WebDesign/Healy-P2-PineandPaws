About Pine & Paws

Gentle care. Professional grooming. Happier pets.

Our Story

Pine & Paws Pet Grooming was established to provide local pet owners with professional grooming in an environment built around patience, cleanliness, and respectful animal handling.

We recognize that every pet responds differently to salon care. Some pets settle in immediately, while others need additional time, gentle breaks, and quiet reassurance.

Our team always prioritizes animal comfort and health. If a requested service or haircut is not safe or suitable for your pet's coat or temperament, we will discuss a more appropriate care plan with you.

Why Choose Us

Our commitment to you and your pet

What Owners Can Expect

When you entrust your pet to Pine & Paws, our team promises to:

  • Treat every pet with patience and gentle handling
  • Maintain a calm, clean, and sanitized salon environment
  • Communicate clearly regarding coat health and service needs
  • Prioritize pet well-being over quick completion times
  • Use safe, stress-reducing grooming techniques

Our Philosophy

We believe grooming should support both long-term health and daily comfort.

Routine salon visits help to:

  • Reduce shedding and loose fur around your home
  • Prevent painful coat matting and tangling
  • Maintain proper nail length for healthy walking posture
  • Spot early changes in skin health, ears, or coat condition

Pet Safety & Preparation

To help us tailor the visit, we encourage owners to share:

  • Existing health conditions or age-related mobility limits
  • Known allergies or skin sensitivities
  • Past grooming anxieties or behavioral concerns
  • Sensitive areas requiring extra gentle handling
