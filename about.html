About Pine & Paws

Gentle care. Professional grooming. Happier pets.

Our Story

Pine & Paws Pet Grooming was established to provide local pet owners with professional grooming in an environment built around patience, cleanliness, and respectful animal handling.

The salon recognizes that pets respond differently to grooming. Some pets are comfortable immediately, while others may need additional time and reassurance.

The staff works to create positive grooming experiences whenever possible so, the salon may recommend a different grooming approach if a requested service would not be safe or appropriate for the pet.

Why Choose Us

Our commitment to you and your pet

Staff Commitment

Pine & Paws Pet Grooming employees are expected to:

  • treat pets respectfully
  • maintain a clean workspace
  • communicate clearly with owners
  • report concerns they observe
  • use safe handling techniques
  • prioritize animal welfare

Our Philosophy

The salon believes grooming should support both appearance and health.

Routine grooming can help:

  • reduce loose fur
  • prevent severe matting
  • keep nails at a comfortable length
  • maintain coat cleanliness
  • improve pet comfort
  • Help owners notice changes in skin or coat condition

Pet Safety

Owners are encouraged to tell staff about:

  • health conditions
  • allergies
  • previous injuries
  • sensitive areas
  • anxiety
  • behavioral concerns
  • previous grooming difficulties
  • any other information that may affect the appointment
  • Groomers work at a pace appropriate for each pet whenever possible
