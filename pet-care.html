Coat Care & Brushing

Regular brushing keeps your pet's coat clean, manageable, and comfortable between salon appointments.

Depending on coat type, routine brushing helps to:

  • Remove loose fur and reduce shedding around the house
  • Prevent painful tangles and severe matting
  • Distribute natural skin oils for a shiny, healthy coat
  • Allow owners to notice early changes in skin health or external parasites

Note: Severe mats should never be pulled or cut at home with scissors, as doing so risks injuring delicate pet skin. Professional assistance is recommended for severe matting.

Nail Maintenance

Keeping nails at a comfortable length helps pets maintain natural posture and walk smoothly without joint strain.

Overgrown nails may lead to:

  • Painful pressure on toe joints while standing or running
  • Nail splitting or painful snagging on rugs and grass
  • Curling back into the paw pad in severe cases

Trimming frequency depends on your pet's age, activity level, and walking surfaces, but typically ranges every 3 to 4 weeks.

Preparing for a Grooming Visit

Help your pet enjoy a calm salon experience by taking a few simple steps before arriving:

  • Allow your pet time for a light bathroom break right before drop-off
  • Inform our team of any medical conditions, recent injuries, or skin sensitivities
  • Share behavioral preferences or past grooming concerns so we can adjust handling
  • Avoid feeding unusually large meals immediately prior to their appointment time

After-Grooming Rest & Care

Some pets experience sensory tiredness after a full grooming session.

  • Provide fresh water and a quiet space to nap after returning home
  • Allow time for skin to adjust if significant matting or heavy coat was removed
  • Contact our salon if you have any questions or notice unexpected skin irritation
