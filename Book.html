Appointment Information

Customers may contact Pine & Paws Pet Grooming by phone or email to request an appointment.

When requesting an appointment, customers should be prepared to provide:

  • Owner name
  • Phone number
  • Pet name
  • Pet type
  • Approximate pet size
  • Requested service
  • Preferred appointment date
  • Any important grooming or health information

Submitting a request does not guarantee a specific appointment time until the salon confirms availability.

How is the final grooming price determined?

Final pricing depends on pet size, coat condition, coat type, temperament, requested services, and the amount of time required to complete the groom safely.

Can I stay with my pet during grooming?

Owners normally do not remain in the grooming area. Some pets become more anxious or distracted when they can see their owners during the grooming process.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. Cat grooming is available by appointment. Service availability may depend on the cat's temperament and grooming needs.

What happens if my pet becomes too stressed?

The groomer may pause, modify, or stop the service if continuing would create excessive stress or a safety concern. The owner will be contacted when appropriate.

How often should my pet be professionally groomed?

The appropriate grooming schedule depends on breed, coat type, lifestyle, health, and home grooming routine. Some pets benefit from professional grooming every four to eight weeks, while others may need appointments less frequently.

What if my pet has severe matting?

The groomer will evaluate the coat. Severe matting may require additional time, special handling, a shorter haircut, or a modified grooming plan. The safety and comfort of the pet will take priority over preserving coat length.
