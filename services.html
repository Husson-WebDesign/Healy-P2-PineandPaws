Our Services

Gentle care. Professional grooming. Happier pets.

Our Services

All prices shown are starting prices.

Final pricing depends on pet size, coat type, coat condition, temperament, requested services, and the amount of grooming time required.

What We Offer

Bath & Brush

A routine maintenance service that includes shampoo, conditioner when appropriate, blow drying, brushing, ear cleaning, and coat finishing.

This service is appropriate for many pets that do not require a haircut.

Starting Price: $35

Approximate Duration: 60-90 minutes

Full Groom

A complete grooming appointment that includes bathing, brushing, drying, nail trimming, ear cleaning, haircut or coat trimming when appropriate, and finishing.

Starting Price: $55

Approximate Duration: 90-150 minutes

Nail Care

Nail trimming and basic nail inspection to help maintain comfortable nail length.

Starting Price: $18

Approximate Duration: 15-20 minutes

De-Shedding Treatment

A specialized coat treatment and brushing service designed to remove loose undercoat and reduce excessive shedding.

This service is especially useful for many double-coated breeds.

Starting Price: $30

Approximate Duration: 45-75 minutes

Puppy Introduction

A short and gentle first grooming experience for young dogs.

The goal is to help create a positive foundation for future grooming appointments.

The appointment may introduce the puppy to:

  • brushing
  • gentle handling
  • grooming tables
  • dryers
  • grooming sounds
  • paw handling
  • and basic salon routines

Starting Price: $30

Approximate Duration: 30-45 minutes

Cat Grooming

Cat grooming services are scheduled by appointment.

Professional grooming services for cats may include:

  • Brushing
  • Coat maintenance
  • Nail trimming
  • Sanitary trimming when appropriate
  • Other services depending on coat condition and temperament

Starting Price: $65

Approximate Duration: 60-120 minutes
