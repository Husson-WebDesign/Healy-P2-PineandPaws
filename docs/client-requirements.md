## 2. Business Overview

Pine & Paws Pet Grooming is a locally operated pet grooming salon serving dogs and cats.

The salon provides professional grooming services in a clean, calm, and safety-focused environment.

The business was created around the idea that grooming should be more than simply improving a pet's appearance. Grooming should also support coat health, comfort, hygiene, and the overall well-being of the animal.

The staff works with pet owners to understand each pet's grooming needs, coat condition, age, temperament, and previous grooming experience.

## Coat Care

Regular brushing helps keep a pet's coat clean and manageable.

Depending on coat type, brushing may help:

* remove loose fur,
* prevent tangles,
* reduce matting,
* distribute natural oils,
* remove small amounts of debris,
* and allow owners to notice changes in the skin or coat.

Owners should use brushes and grooming tools appropriate for their pet's coat type.

Severe mats should not be pulled or cut at home without proper knowledge because doing so may injure the skin.

---

## Nail Care

Regular nail maintenance helps pets remain comfortable while standing and walking.

Overgrown nails may:

* affect a pet's walking posture,
* become uncomfortable,
* snag on surfaces,
* split,
* or curl toward the paw.

How often nails need trimming depends on the pet's activity level, age, and how quickly the nails grow.

---

## Preparing for a Groom

Before an appointment, owners should:

* allow their pet to use the bathroom,
* arrive at the scheduled appointment time,
* inform staff about medical conditions,
* report allergies or skin sensitivities,
* explain behavioral concerns,
* mention any recent injuries,
* and tell the groomer about previous grooming difficulties.

Owners should avoid giving pets unusually large meals immediately before a grooming appointment.

---

## After-Groom Care

Some pets may be tired after grooming.

After returning home:

* provide fresh water,
* allow the pet time to rest,
* monitor the skin for unexpected irritation,
* avoid excessive outdoor activity immediately if the pet appears tired,
* and contact the salon if there are concerns about the grooming service.

Pets that have had significant matting removed may temporarily behave differently because their skin and movement feel different after grooming.

# 17. Frequently Asked Questions

The following questions and answers are approved for website use.

---

### How is the final grooming price determined?

Final pricing depends on pet size, coat condition, coat type, temperament, requested services, and the amount of time required to complete the groom safely.

---

### Can I stay with my pet during grooming?

Owners normally do not remain in the grooming area.

Some pets become more anxious or distracted when they can see their owners during the grooming process.

---

### Do you groom cats?

Yes. Cat grooming is available by appointment.

Service availability may depend on the cat's temperament and grooming needs.

---

### What happens if my pet becomes too stressed?

The groomer may pause, modify, or stop the service if continuing would create excessive stress or a safety concern.

The owner will be contacted when appropriate.

---

### How often should my pet be professionally groomed?

The appropriate grooming schedule depends on breed, coat type, lifestyle, health, and home grooming routine.

Some pets benefit from professional grooming every four to eight weeks, while others may need appointments less frequently.

---

### What if my pet has severe matting?

The groomer will evaluate the coat.

Severe matting may require additional time, special handling, a shorter haircut, or a modified grooming plan.

The safety and comfort of the pet will take priority over preserving coat length.
